"Hey there! I'm planning a corporate outing for about 45 people during the Stampede. We want to see the Evening Show but also want a private space with catering. What do you recommend?"

For a group of 45 looking for a private space with catering for the Evening Show, I highly recommend our premium Infield Suites—they sit right in the heart of the action with private catering and a spectacular view of the chuckwagons and fireworks.

Alternatively, we could look at a semi-private Reserved Table Bundle at Ranchman's. Let me check the live Salesforce inventory for you. Do you have a specific date in mind, or are you flexible?"

"We're looking at July 9th or 10th. By the way, is there a dress code for the Infield Suites? Some of our clients are coming straight from a conference."

"No dress code to worry about! While many folks love to wear their favorite western boots and cowboy hats, business casual or conference attire is perfectly fine. We want everyone to feel comfortable!

Now, looking at our live Salesforce inventory for July 9th and 10th: I see we have an Infield Suite available on July 9th, but July 10th is currently sold out. For July 10th, we do have semi-private tables at Ranchman's.

Would you like me to create a high-priority corporate lead in Salesforce for the July 9th Infield Suite so our VIP coordinator can lock it in for you?"

Implementation Roadmap

1. Setup Directories

Configure package.json files and install dependencies for both frontend and backend.

2. Implement Salesforce Client

Write the JSforce connection module with robust mock fallback database for easy offline presentation.

3. Build MCP Server

Expose tools (get_event_catalog, check_ticket_availability, create_private_event_lead) using official MCP SDK.

4. Create Conversational Frontend

Build the React website and Agentforce widget with custom NLU simulator for context-switching and slot-filling.

Future Agentic Connectivity (MCP + Gemini)

By implementing the Model Context Protocol, we can allow external LLMs (like Google Gemini) to securely query and interact with our Salesforce data. Here is an example of the tools our MCP server will register:

get_event_catalog()

Queries Salesforce for active Stampede events, descriptions, pricing, and venue capacities.

check_ticket_availability(date)

Checks real-time ticket inventory and venue availability in Salesforce for a given date.

create_private_event_lead(...)

Inserts a high-priority corporate lead record directly into Salesforce CRM with extracted details.
